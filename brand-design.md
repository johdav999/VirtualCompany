# Wellheld brand design

## Brand idea

Wellheld should express **calm operational intelligence**: warm enough to feel human and structured enough to be trusted with finance and company operations.

The central visual story is important work arriving, becoming organized, and remaining under clear human control. The brand should avoid the visual language of AI spectacle. Wellheld is an operating team and governance system, not a chatbot or a futuristic assistant.

Recommended direction: **Quiet governance**. The reference point is Nordic business software combined with the clarity and restraint of a well-designed financial publication.

## Brand characteristics

- Calm, capable, and reassuring
- Precise without feeling bureaucratic
- Human without resembling healthcare or wellness branding
- Modern without neon gradients, robots, holograms, or AI sparkles
- Premium while remaining accessible to small-company owners

## Name and wordmark

Use **Wellheld** as one word in title case.

The wordmark should use a humanist grotesk with open letterforms, slightly softened corners, and strong lowercase readability. Avoid excessively circular startup typography, futuristic cuts, and monospaced styling.

For presentations and current product materials, use **Segoe UI** as the practical brand typeface. It provides the required humanist character and is broadly available in Microsoft environments.

Suggested hierarchy:

| Use | Weight | Typical size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Presentation cover | Bold | 48–68 pt |
| Presentation title | Semibold or bold | 32–40 pt |
| Section heading | Semibold | 20–24 pt |
| Body copy | Regular | 17–20 pt |
| Labels and notes | Semibold or regular | 11–15 pt |

## Logo concept

The preferred symbol uses two open structural forms holding a central element:

```text
┌     ┐
   ●
└     ┘
```

The open frame communicates connection and activity while the central element communicates a company, decision, or piece of work held within a dependable operating structure.

Possible meanings include:

- Human supervision around AI activity
- Multiple business functions brought into one coherent place
- Guardrails that organize work without creating confinement
- A company held within a dependable operating structure

A secondary exploration may use a custom `WH` monogram with open outer strokes. Avoid literal hands, hugs, shields, buildings, and robot heads.

## Colour system

| Role | Colour | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Held Navy | `#17233B` | Primary brand colour, wordmark, headings, navigation |
| Clear Blue | `#356AE6` | Intelligence, action, and continuity with the product |
| Warm Paper | `#F6F3ED` | Human warmth, editorial backgrounds, and brand fields |
| Quiet White | `#FCFCFB` | Product surfaces and light presentation canvases |
| Moss | `#4F7865` | Healthy, completed, and stable states |
| Amber | `#C98528` | Attention, review, and visible human decisions |
| Soft Ink | `#4C5567` | Secondary text and supporting information |

Warm Paper is a key part of the identity. It separates Wellheld from generic blue-and-white enterprise software and gives the system a considered, dependable tone.

Green should remain a supporting status colour rather than the dominant brand colour. A green-led identity would move the name toward wellness, caregiving, or sustainability.

## Graphic language: held fields

The main graphic device is an open frame around important information. Frames may expand to accept new work, connect when agents coordinate, contain a decision awaiting approval, or open when work is completed or handed back to a person.

Use held fields sparingly. A frame should identify the part of the composition that matters, not decorate every block. Prefer flat compositions with generous spacing over dense card grids or dashboard-like mosaics.

## Layout and composition

- Use a 16:9 canvas for presentations.
- Build layouts on generous outer margins with clear, asymmetric balance.
- Allow large areas of Warm Paper or Quiet White to remain empty.
- Use one primary composition per page or slide.
- Place the most important statement first, then support it with a small number of concrete details.
- Use Held Navy for hierarchy, Clear Blue for active structure, and Amber only where human attention is required.
- Prefer subtle paper texture and restrained geometry over shadows, glass effects, and gradients.

## Photography

Show real owners and operators at moments of regained attention. Suitable scenes include reviewing one clear decision, speaking with an employee or customer, working in a genuine small-company environment, or closing a laptop after the work is under control.

Avoid people pointing excitedly at graphs, generic call-centre teams, soft-focus caregiving imagery, humanoid robots, holographic interfaces, and hands cradling objects.

## Illustration

Use structured editorial diagrams with the visual quality of a modern annual report. Suitable subjects include documents becoming reviewed work, specialist roles around a shared company record, visible approval points, and coordinated finance, sales, marketing, and support activity.

Illustrations should use crisp geometry, restrained colour, light paper texture, and clear negative space. They should not resemble a product dashboard unless the work specifically calls for a product screenshot.

## Agent identities

Named agents add personality inside the Wellheld system without becoming separate cartoon mascots.

| Agent | Function | Secondary colour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Laura | Finance | Clear Blue |
| Alex | Sales | Amber |
| Maya | Marketing | Restrained coral |
| Ben | Support | Restrained teal |

Portraits should share one consistent photographic or illustration treatment. Masterbrand navy and paper tones should remain dominant around them.

## Motion

Motion should show work settling into order:

1. Separate pieces of company work enter the composition.
2. The pieces align into a clear operating structure.
3. A human decision point remains visually distinct.
4. The composition becomes still.

Use short movements with gentle deceleration. Avoid endless pulsing, autonomous swarms, glowing particles, and dramatic generation effects. Wellheld should make the screen feel quieter as it processes work.

## Voice and copy

Use direct, grounded language. Explain what the system does, where the evidence comes from, and what remains under the user's control.

Preferred homepage expression:

> **Your company, well held.**  
> A governed AI team for finance, sales, marketing and support, working under your direction.

Avoid inflated AI claims, vague transformation language, and descriptions of autonomous magic. Prefer concrete terms such as review, evidence, approval, responsibility, source, decision, and handoff.

## Presentation application

Wellheld presentations should use Warm Paper or Quiet White backgrounds, Held Navy titles, Clear Blue structure, and Amber for decisions or review points. Use large editorial illustrations on only one side of a slide and keep the opposite side open for concise copy.

Cover slides should remain minimal. Content slides should use one clear argument rather than a collection of miniature interface panels. Body text should remain at least 17 pt, with presentation titles at least 32 pt.

## Accessibility

- Maintain strong contrast between text and backgrounds.
- Do not rely on colour alone to communicate status.
- Keep body text at presentation-safe sizes.
- Add useful alternative text to meaningful images.
- Keep decorative colour accents secondary to written labels and hierarchy.
- Use plain language and short paragraphs that can be understood without presenter narration.

## Brand guardrails

Use:

- Warm editorial backgrounds
- Open frames and controlled structure
- Evidence, review, and human approval as visual themes
- Quiet, deliberate motion
- Real operating contexts and restrained diagrams

Avoid:

- Neon gradients and dark futuristic control rooms
- AI sparkles, glowing brains, robots, and holograms
- Giant chatbot windows as the main product expression
- Playful agent mascots that compete with the masterbrand
- Dense card grids, excessive pills, and decorative status badges
- Wellness, caregiving, or sustainability cues as the dominant identity
