# Swedish chart and VAT research inventory

Research date: 2026-08-27.

This inventory records authoritative inputs discovered for the Swedish accounting and VAT domain. Only the two rules in `vat-rules.json` are enabled by specification 2026.1. Every other item is research material for a separately reviewed successor version and must remain blocked.

## Promotion rule

An inventory item may be promoted into `vat-rules.json` only in a new version after its exact transaction predicates, evidence, effective dates, BAS mappings, VAT-box facts, invoice requirements, correction behavior, and positive/negative fixtures have been approved. A source citation or rate alone is not a complete deterministic rule.
