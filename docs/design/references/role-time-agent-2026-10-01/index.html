The recommended product model

Use one consistent company workspace, shaped by responsibility, time horizon and the decision at hand. Keep module navigation stable. Change the priorities, evidence and actions within the workspace. A person who is both CEO and sales owner should be able to switch perspective without changing identity or gaining permissions.

Every operational screen should answer: What changed? Why does it matter now? What needs my decision? What are the agents doing about it? What happens next?

The design builds on the current Today and Monthly workspaces, responsibility assignments, Agent team, Work center, effective-authority presentation, and existing finance grants. Week, Quarter, Year and 3 years are proposed extensions. All nine images are design concepts with fictional data; none demonstrates a working feature or a live business result.

The canonical product specification remains docs/design.md. Its application colors and Inter typography govern these references. The calmer tone in brand-design.md informs the composition, but this proposal does not silently replace the application's design tokens or wordmark.

Workspace structure and relevance

  1. Stable navigation: Overview, Agent team, Finance, Sales, Marketing, Support, Work; History and Settings below. Keep detailed workflows in their existing module. Marketing is present in the current NavMenu even though the short primary-navigation list in design.md omits it; reconcile that documentation during implementation.
  2. Explicit context: company, perspective, responsibility scope (Mine / My team / Company where permitted), period and comparison. Persist personal preferences separately from authority. Use the existing lens keys: company, sales, marketing, finance and customers.
  3. Situation summary: two or three grounded sentences with an as-of time, source coverage, and a visible indication of preliminary or incomplete information.
  4. Attention list: three to five priorities, each showing what happened, impact, accountable person, working agent, due time, evidence and one next action. “See all” preserves the complete queue.
  5. Role workspace: a deal queue, campaign plan, close checklist or case queue, plus three or four useful measures. Each measure needs a definition, unit, period, comparison and drill-down.
  6. Contextual rail: show the selected item's decision, agent contribution and evidence. Summarize other approvals as a count linked to Work. The same approval must not become several independent tasks.

Use responsibility and authorization to select eligible information before ranking it. Among eligible items, prioritize critical incidents and expiring decisions, then personal accountability, deadline, material impact and work blocked for other people. Collapse duplicate signals about the same business event. Include “Why this is here”, such as “You own receivables; three invoices are overdue”. Let users pin, snooze and tune noncritical reminders, while clearly showing when a critical alert cannot be suppressed by that preference.

Do not reorder rows under the pointer or move keyboard focus when live information changes. Show “3 updates available” and apply them at a safe moment. Summaries can be AI-written, but money, dates, states, permissions and priority reasons must be sourced from structured records. Missing data is “Unavailable”, not zero or healthy.

Responsibility is not a security role. A “CEO” perspective is not automatic access to every personnel, finance or customer record. Enforce company and record permissions in the API, including counts, previews, exports, cached summaries and notifications. Where the viewer cannot access evidence, show an authorized status and owner without leaking the protected content.

Time changes the question, not just the date filter

Now: what needs intervention? Today: what must get done? Week: what should move or be learned? Month: what results and variances need explaining? Quarter: where should resources change? Year: what commitments and obligations do we own? Several years: which direction is viable, under what assumptions?

Clock times are configurable defaults, not universal truths. A desk-based profile might use a start-of-day briefing, a midday check and a day-end handover. Support should follow shifts and on-call ownership. Sales should follow meetings and commitments. Finance follows close dates and payment cutoffs. Show agent work as business outcomes

Provide three levels of detail: a compact outcome on the role home; a task or initiative card in Agent team; and a detail view with outputs, evidence, handoffs, approvals and history. Keep Work as the shared human action inbox. Chat remains a way to ask about work, not its authoritative status record.

Retain the existing Agent team columns Planned, In progress, Waiting for human approval and Completed. Add filters or explicit substates for Waiting for data, Waiting for another agent, Blocked, Failed, Paused and Reconciliation needed. Do not hide failures inside In progress or label an ambiguous external result Completed. If a card has remained unchanged beyond its expected interval, show that it needs attention rather than animating a spinner indefinitely.

Use “3 of 5 checks completed” only when those checks are defined. Avoid invented progress percentages and uncalibrated confidence scores. Explain evidence quality with practical language: “Two current sources support this”, “Contract missing”, or “Estimate based on last month's figures”. Distinguish output produced, external delivery verified and business result achieved.

For CEOs, aggregate outcomes, exposure and decisions. For functional managers, show workload, blockers and quality. For operators, show the exact artifact and next action. For administrators, keep technical execution evidence available in a separate diagnostic view. Measure verified outcomes, cycle time, blocked time, corrections, reopens, intervention effort and policy incidents. Do not reward agents for message counts, token use or tool calls.

Visualize collaboration around one shared goal

Keep the Agent team board for portfolio-level work. Open a selected initiative into a simple swimlane or dependency flow. Each lane is an accountable role or specialist; each node is a work step; each arrow identifies the artifact or decision handed over. Use a small number of meaningful nodes and collapse technical substeps. Also provide a chronological list for narrow screens and assistive technology.

The renewal reference shows a concrete handoff: Sales prepares the brief; Finance checks the margin; Support provides open-case history; Sales revises the proposal; a human reviews terms; the approved delivery is dispatched. A dependency should say what is awaited, who owns it, when it is due, and what will happen if it fails.

Show disagreements as decision evidence: “Sales requested 12%; Finance recommends 8% because of margin policy.” Preserve the alternatives, cited sources and final decision. Do not average conflicting recommendations into a misleading consensus. An unresolved conflict has a named human decision owner and deadline. Show concise rationale summaries and source material, not hidden model reasoning or raw inter-agent chatter.

Use existing task, workflow, plan, subtask, approval and correlation identifiers to join the view. Add explicit dependency/output references where absent; do not infer handoffs from matching text. One shared initiative must not count as three company outcomes merely because three agents contributed. Permission-filter the flow and its artifacts.

Autonomy: a four-position selector with explicit scope

Use the gear-stick idea as four named positions, implemented as accessible radio buttons or a segmented control. A speedometer suggests that more is always better; an unlabelled slider suggests precision that the policy does not have. Keep a separate, always understandable pause control. Do not add an unrestricted “fully autonomous” mode.

PositionUser promiseCompany-level semantic mapping
1 · AdviseInspect allowed information, recommend and prepare proposed outputs for review; no autonomous business changesRecommend
2 · OrganizeArrange validated low-risk internal work into tasks and plans within the configured scopeOrganize
3 · Do internal workRun permitted internal tasks; external actions and protected internal changes still require their policy checksOperateInternally
4 · Act within limitsExecute only explicitly granted actions, targets and limits; required approval remains requiredControlledExecution; not a blanket external-execution grant

These labels correspond to the existing company-level concepts. They are not a numeric conversion for AgentAutonomyLevel or FinanceAutonomyLevel. Finance already distinguishes read/monitor, recommend/draft, supervised internal execution and scheduled bounded execution. Resolve actual behavior through the owning policies and present a capability-level explanation; preserve persisted values and existing permissions.

  1. Company: a default operating posture, an explicit maximum envelope, non-overridable requirements, and pause/emergency stop. Only authorized administrators change these.
  2. Task type or capability: the main everyday configuration. Separate research, drafting, sending, posting, payment release, publishing, refunds and budget changes. A grant can differ from the default only within the company envelope and the grantor's authority.
  3. Agent: available tools, data scopes, status and capabilities can further constrain a task. Reassigning or delegating must not expand authority.
  4. Task instance: allow “Require review for this case” or a more restrictive amount/recipient scope. One approved action must not become a permanent grant.

The effective permission is the intersection of the applicable action grants, user/agent permissions, target scopes, company limits and domain rules, with explicit denials taking precedence. Mandatory approval is an additional gate. Avoid computing it by taking the minimum of unrelated enum integers. Show both the configured level and the effective result: “Configured: Act within limits. Effective: review required because this customer has an open dispute.”

Each grant should expose the allowed action, recipients/records, amount and aggregate budget where relevant, volume/rate limits, sources and freshness, operating hours, expiry, approval rule, escalation owner and change history. Prevent splitting work across agents or retries to bypass limits. Use atomic budget reservation and a consistent currency policy for financial limits.

Before increasing autonomy, show the exact before/after change and an impact preview using historical cases or a dry run. The preview must execute no external actions and must distinguish simulated eligibility from prediction. Require an authorized user to apply the grant and record a version, reason and expiry. Use a limited pilot with review sampling. Good past performance may prompt a suggestion, but must never silently increase authority.

Recheck current authority, evidence, approval and limits immediately before execution, including delayed jobs and retries. Material changes to amount, recipient or payload invalidate the previous approval. A global pause blocks new execution and brings safe in-flight steps to a stop; show actions already sent, actions stopping, and actions requiring reconciliation. Do not promise that pausing reverses a payment or recalls a delivered message. Resuming also needs current policy evaluation.

Keep operational pause distinct from shutting off read-only monitoring and alerts. The scope should be explicit on the button and confirmation/result state. Department owners should only control their authorized area; a support lead must not be able to lower company-wide financial controls.

Acceptance and visual implementation

  • A user with two responsibilities can switch perspective and keep a stable navigation; their accessible records never expand merely by choosing another view.
  • A stale or disconnected source produces a visible coverage warning and unavailable measures; no fabricated summary or zero-value substitution appears.
  • The same approval opened from Today, Work or a collaboration flow resolves to the same durable record and current state.
  • A grant revoked after a task was queued is re-evaluated before the external action, and execution stops with an actionable explanation.
  • A paused agent cannot evade the pause by delegating to another agent; already completed external actions remain visible and are not presented as undone.
  • A monthly close displays preliminary versus finalized data, correct period comparisons, and links to evidence. Annual targets and scenario outputs cannot be confused with recorded results.
  • At 1440px desktop, 768px tablet and approximately 390px mobile, preserve context, priorities and decisions. Collapse the right rail into contextual panels; offer a list alternative to flow diagrams and dense tables.
  • All autonomy settings work with keyboard and click/tap without dragging. Status has words/icons as well as color. Announce meaningful updates without stealing focus; respect reduced motion.
  • In role-based usability sessions, participants can identify the most consequential action, explain why it is relevant, locate its evidence, and correctly state what an agent may do. Measure errors and understanding as well as speed.

For each implemented screen, follow the repository's reference-first workflow, compare the real UI to its reference, and test real states as well as the ideal populated state: loading, empty, partial data, no permission, waiting, failure and recovery. The textual specifications and authoritative design rules take precedence over generated-image mistakes.

Sources and design rationale

The detailed product recommendations are design judgments tailored to this repository. The underlying interaction principles are supported by the following primary sources, consulted 1 October 2026.

  • Microsoft Research: Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction — set appropriate expectations, make behavior understandable and support correction over time.
  • Google PAIR: Feedback + Control — balance automation and user control, make feedback and its consequences understandable.
  • W3C: Dragging Movements — provide a non-drag pointer method for controls that would otherwise require dragging.
  • W3C: Status Messages — expose relevant status updates to assistive technology without requiring focus changes.
