# Responsibility-driven Monthly workspace reference prompt

Use case: ui-mockup

Asset type: production-quality responsive SaaS dashboard reference, showing a
desktop Overview workspace and a narrow mobile adaptation side by side.

Primary request: Design the Monthly period of Virtual Company's canonical
Overview experience. It must visibly share the established Today workspace shell
while reading as a calm monthly management review rather than a daily alert
dashboard.

Existing product context: Virtual Company is an executive control center where
AI agents operate Finance, Sales, Marketing, and Customer Support while a human
owner or manager supervises decisions. The dark navy 240px sidebar contains
Overview, Agent team, Finance, Sales, Support, Work, History, and Settings. Use the
existing white/light-blue SaaS surface, Inter-like typography, blue primary
actions, 12px cards, subtle borders and shadows, and restrained green, amber, and
red status accents.

Desktop layout:

- Dark navy product sidebar at left, a spacious main workspace in the center,
  and a narrow decision/agent follow-up rail at right.
- Header title "Monthly review", company "Atlas Co.", reporting period
  "August 1–31, 2026", comparison "vs July 2026", generated/freshness state, and
  compact previous/next month controls.
- A clear Today / Monthly segmented period switch above the familiar
  responsibility lens tabs: Company, Finance, Sales, Marketing, Customers.
- A concise deterministic management summary beneath the tabs, including source
  coverage such as "4 of 5 sources current".
- Four high-signal monthly result cards only: Revenue, Net result, Pipeline
  movement, and SLA performance. Each includes an honest comparison or an
  "Unavailable" setup state; do not use decorative charts.
- A prominent "Next month priorities" section with three ranked action cards.
  Each card explains what changed, why it matters, owner/agent, and the canonical
  workflow action.
- Stacked feature review cards for Finance, Sales, Marketing, Customer Support,
  and Company operation. Each uses a short monthly outcome statement, 2–4
  compact facts, exceptions or source coverage, and one "Open workspace" action.
  Do not copy all daily cards or create a passive chart grid.
- Right rail with "Decisions before next month" and "Agent outcomes" cards.
  Decisions have Review actions; agent rows clearly distinguish completed,
  blocked, and recommended states.
- Include an honest partial-data note for one unavailable marketing outcome with
  a setup/action path instead of a fabricated value.

Mobile layout: Show the same route and context in a single column. Keep the Today
/ Monthly switch and horizontally scrollable lens tabs near the top. Stack the
summary, two-column result cards, priority cards, feature review accordions,
decisions, and agent outcomes. Preserve clear tap targets and avoid horizontal
page overflow.

Style/medium: realistic shippable B2B SaaS product UI, not concept art; crisp,
quiet, accessible, action-oriented, and information-dense without feeling busy.

Color palette: #F7F9FC background, #FFFFFF cards, #2563EB primary, #16A34A
success, #F59E0B warning, #DC2626 danger, navy sidebar, dark slate body text.

Text (verbatim where practical): "Monthly review", "Today", "Monthly",
"August 1–31, 2026", "vs July 2026", "Next month priorities",
"Decisions before next month", "Agent outcomes", "Open workspace",
"Unavailable", "Set up source".

Constraints: maintain the recognizable Today interaction model; prioritize
actions and responsible people over charts; use plain English; show no invented
profitability-by-customer, payroll, or pricing metrics; no unrelated navigation;
no logos other than a simple abstract Virtual Company product mark; no watermark.
