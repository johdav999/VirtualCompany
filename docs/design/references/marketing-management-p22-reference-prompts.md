Heading "Budget proposal", subtitle "Planning assumptions; actual campaign limits require a separate controlled workflow". Main two-column form/results layout: company, selected month, one currency, accountable reviewer and retained revision metadata at top. Primary button "Save proposal" or "Refresh as new revision"; plain-language validation that allocations equal proposed total and currencies match. Results card shows original allocation totals, differences only where current budgets exist, retained report/model evidence, snapshot identity and notes. Lower saved revisions list with "Open retained revision". Maya's context rail recommends checking missing-cost evidence. Empty history state is explicit. Responsive layout stacks the same form and results. No fake spending execution or approval button.
