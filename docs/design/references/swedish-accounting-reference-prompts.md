# Swedish accounting Release 1 reference prompts

These reference prompts were written and generated before the Prompt 6 UI implementation. They follow `docs/design.md` and `ui-instructions.md`; the images are design references only and are not shipped as application assets.

## Swedish accounting setup

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: high-fidelity desktop SaaS product screenshot. Create the materially extended “Set up Swedish accounting” screen for the existing Virtual Company finance product. Preserve the current app shell: fixed white left sidebar, Finance top navigation, light #F7F9FC workspace, Inter-style typography, blue #2563EB accents, white 14–16px rounded cards, soft borders and restrained shadows. At 1536×1024, show a grouped setup flow with a compact left step rail: Legal identity, Accounting settings, Swedish chart, VAT registration, Document series, Review. The main workspace shows the Legal identity step with editable business facts, registered address, organisation number, VAT status, fiscal year and bookkeeping method grouped into clear cards. Across the top, show three distinct readiness cards labeled exactly “Format complete”, “Confirmed by your team”, and “Independent review”, with the last state visibly “Pending review” and no government seal or compliance claim. Include one amber attention summary naming missing business facts and one safe primary action. On the right, keep Laura visible as “Laura, Finance Manager” with a grounded explanation, source-navigation links, and a “Message Laura” action; she must not claim validation. Include a compact setup preview and final confirmation area. Show production-shaped loading/empty/error affordances only where natural, accessible form labels, obvious keyboard focus, and a narrow responsive preview inset. Avoid raw policy keys, hashes, internal reason codes, mock data banners, simulation controls, dense technical terminology, futuristic styling, gradients, or decorative charts. Render realistic product UI, not concept art; use legible English copy and no watermark.

## VAT and statutory reporting

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: high-fidelity desktop SaaS product screenshot. Create the integrated “VAT & statutory exports” workspace inside the existing Virtual Company Accounting reports surface. Preserve the current app shell and visual language: fixed white left sidebar, Finance/Accounting navigation, light #F7F9FC background, Inter-style type, blue #2563EB accents, white rounded cards, soft borders and subtle shadows. At 1536×1024, show a period selector and readiness summary at the top, then accessible tabs or pills for “VAT return”, “Sources & reconciliation”, “Corrections”, and “Statutory exports”. The VAT return view must show a clear lifecycle status, total payable or refundable VAT, approval requirements, stale-state messaging, blocking issues, and one authoritative next action derived from backend permissions. Show readable VAT box results in a practical table, a source drill-down panel with navigable journal/document references, and ledger reconciliation. Include review, request approval, finalize, and package download controls with unavailable actions visibly explained. Add a statutory export card for SIE and accounting archive jobs showing queued/running/failed/completed, progress, retry, expiry and download behavior. On the right, keep Laura visible as “Laura, Finance Manager” with grounded explanations and source links, while making it clear that Laura cannot finalize the return. Include an intentional empty state and a narrow responsive preview inset. Use plain business English; avoid raw storage states, tax rule keys, pack hashes, internal reason codes, compliance badges, government seals, mock production data, simulation controls, or futuristic styling. Render realistic product UI, not concept art; no watermark.
