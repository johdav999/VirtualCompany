# Finance account-administration reference prompt

Use case: ui-mockup

Asset type: visual target for the Virtual Company Blazor account-lifecycle and accounting-series administration workspace

Primary request: Create a polished desktop SaaS accounting workspace for safely governing chart-of-account lifecycles, reporting controls, replacement accounts, and voucher/document series policies. The screen must make dependency impact and the unsupported inventory boundary immediately understandable to an accountant.

Scene/backdrop: Virtual Company executive control-center application with a 240px left navigation, a spacious central Finance workspace, and a compact right-side Finance Manager insight panel.

Subject: The central workspace has a header titled "Account administration", KPI cards for active accounts, scheduled retirements, unexplained voucher gaps, and series-policy health, followed by tabs for Accounts, Series policies, and Commerce boundary. The Accounts view shows a dense account table and a selected-account governance panel with reportability, posting restriction, effective dates, lifecycle history, replacement-account selector, dependency impact preview, warning states, and a safe "Apply lifecycle change" action. The Series policies view configures voucher or statutory-document series by source, transaction type, fiscal year, location/dimension, jurisdiction, policy pack, and provider series mapping while surfacing version and gap evidence. The Commerce boundary view clearly states that versioned commerce facts are accepted but inventory quantity, valuation, and COGS accounting are unsupported and remain external.

Style/medium: High-fidelity modern enterprise SaaS UI mockup, clean and restrained, matching an accountant-grade control center rather than a marketing dashboard.

Composition/framing: Wide 16:9 desktop composition. Fixed left sidebar, central content with cards, tabs, table and split detail layout, narrow right insight panel. Dense sticky-header table, 12px rounded cards, 16px internal padding, strong visual hierarchy, responsive-friendly grouping.

Lighting/mood: Bright light-mode interface, calm, trustworthy, precise.

Color palette: Background #F7F9FC, white cards, primary #2563EB, success #16A34A, warning #F59E0B, danger #DC2626, dark slate text.

Text (verbatim): "Account administration", "Accounts", "Series policies", "Commerce boundary", "Impact preview", "Replacement account", "Apply lifecycle change", "Inventory accounting is not supported".

Constraints: Inter typography; headings at 600 weight; subtle shadows; readable tables; plain-English labels; prominent safe next action; never suggest deletion or merge; no raw GUIDs; include normal and exception states in one coherent screen.

Avoid: decorative gradients, oversized charts, consumer-finance styling, cramped controls, unlabeled icons, dark mode, watermarks, inventory quantity controls.

Generation mode: built-in image generation.
