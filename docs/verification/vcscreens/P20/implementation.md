# P20 monthly reviews and retained revisions

P20 extends the existing Monthly Overview in place. Today, Week, native business commands, financial statement snapshots and operating cycle snapshots retain their owners. Operations owns the management-review aggregate; module contributors remain the source owners.

The review table includes every authorized Monthly measure, its captured actual or forecast value, prior value, definition, unit, owning source, matching active monthly CompanyGoal and goal version, signed prior change and target variance. A goal matches only the exact company-local monthly UTC boundaries, metric key and unit. Annual goals, budgets, multiple matching goals and different currencies are not silently turned into targets. Materiality remains a review judgment; no unsupported threshold is invented.

Finance retains ledger/operational calculations, separate currencies, and recorded fiscal-period close/last-validation facts with the native close action. This management review does not certify accounting readiness or statutory approval. Support's first-response denominator now includes overdue unanswered cases and excludes future unanswered deadlines at the observation cutoff. Sales uses retained, non-deleted stage activities and persisted forecast snapshots; forecast remains distinct from actual, comparison currency must agree, and current pipeline facts separate currencies. Company Work counts describe a bounded currently authorized cohort and suppress protected collaboration ancestry; current risks and missing historical balance coverage are disclosed.

`monthly_review_snapshots` retains a private reviewer history with company, creator, series, predecessor, revision, request identity, lens, year/month, as-of/save timestamps, current-scope fingerprint, immutable bounded JSON input values/results/definitions/coverage/notes/difference explanation, calculation version and SHA-256. The selected filters are responsibility and calendar month; no hidden report filters are inferred. Maximum retained payload is 512 KiB measured as UTF-8; review notes are limited to 2,000 characters. No automatic purge or individual update/delete is exposed. Company deletion is the retention boundary.

Save captures fresh authorized owners instead of the dashboard cache. Retry identities return the original retained record and reject mismatched command meaning. Refresh requires the expected predecessor revision and appends a new record. Unique request, series/revision and non-null predecessor indexes prevent duplicate saves and competing forks across processes. Reopening reproduces the stored payload and validates its integrity, identity, supported calculation version and deterministic change/variance from retained inputs. New sources never overwrite an old revision.

Current membership, Finance policy, responsibility assignment and native Work/transitive source access are rechecked. History is private to the original reviewer; a changed responsibility/role scope can withhold earlier reviews. All Work inputs included in saved counts are retained as scope references, including inputs outside the visible five outcome links. Deleted native records do not erase retained input values; existing restricted records withhold the review. No saved permission grants access.

API: company-scoped `/api/companies/{company}/workspace/monthly/reviews`; GET bounded history, POST save, POST `{id}/open`, `{id}/refresh` and `{id}/export`. Open/export are explicit reproduction commands so failures can persist business audit without adding mutation to a read query. Save/refresh audit commits atomically with the review. Reproduction failures return 422 and audit the safe reason; conflicts return 409. All responses disable caching.

The typed Web client carries company headers and validates company/lens/period/revision identity. `snapshot` on the existing Monthly URL survives reload and exact owning-source returns. Selecting a saved record replaces the displayed monthly results, facts, decisions and outcomes with captured values. Save/history/refresh/export expose loading, empty, access-loss, integrity/error/retry and conflict feedback; notes and retry identities persist. Cancellation and location/generation checks suppress late responses after company changes or departure. Export freshly authorizes and reconciles its rows to the selected retained review. Blob handoff is not physical file-save evidence.
