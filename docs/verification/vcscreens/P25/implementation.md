# P25 ownership and behavior

Operations owns `IQuarterlyPlanningService`, its authorized API and the Quarter overview/detail UI. Native CompanyGoal and OperatingInitiative identities are reused; short operating cycles are not relabeled as quarterly or annual plans. Quarter-specific accountable owners, baseline/target/unit/direction, milestones, measure links, initiative links and resource allocation assumptions are relational children of append-only reviewed revisions. Global objectives retain their native lifecycle and editor. Review notes retain outlook and coordinated decisions.

The company fiscal calendar and timezone establish a frozen period/version/currency binding. Goal version, unit, active owner and initiative goal/company are validated. Native dependencies can cross operating plans; incomplete/blocked/late dependencies remain visible. Named capacity pools share their declared capacity; inconsistent availability uses the smallest assumption and warns, and overcommitment reports the shortfall before save. A conflicted proposal can be retained as a reviewed planning decision with visible warnings. It grants no execution authority.

Quarter actuals sum only `finance.revenue`, `finance.net_result`, `sales.stage_movement` and `support.volume`, with three distinct contiguous retained calendar months wholly covering the quarter and compatible units. Ratios, forecasts, missing months and non-month-start fiscal quarters do not receive invented actuals. Progress is `(actual - baseline) / (target - baseline) × 100`; equal baseline/target leaves progress unavailable. Target direction controls outcome; below-target actuals become missed only after quarter end. Missing/protected actual evidence never becomes a missed-target conclusion.

Each source is opened through the native P20 reproduction command, including current ownership/responsibility/department checks. Its checksum is bound to the immutable relational measure/resource link. Every reopen rechecks access and reproduction; protected values/links are withheld while the reviewed planning assumptions remain readable by authorized company planners. Native snapshots are not copied into a shared JSON blob. Non-additive and incomplete evidence remains explicit.

Unique company/year/quarter/revision, predecessor and request indexes enforce durable concurrency. A stable retry reopens the same revision; request reuse with changed inputs fails. Stale predecessors, goal versions or preview fingerprints fail. Reviewed versions retain their original target/owner/milestones/resources and native calendar dates; diffs explain target, owner, milestone, resources and outlook changes. Native goals, actuals, monthly payloads and execution policies are preserved.

Migration `20261006141229_AddQuarterlyPlanning` adds six owned tables and company-bound alternate keys/FKs on existing goals, initiatives and monthly snapshots. UTC value conversion restores UTC kind on reload without altering the SQL datetime representation. SQL checks verify down/up preservation and concurrent successors plus foreign-source FK rejection.
