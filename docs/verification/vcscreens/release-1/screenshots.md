Comparison: shared navy navigation, white panels, blue actions and source/owner/date evidence are retained. Layout follows the implemented common shell instead of duplicating illustrative controls or outcomes. Sales title compression is corrected; missing cash/approved plans/knowledge and unconfirmed delivery remain visible rather than copying the reference's illustrative favorable numbers. Earlier confirmed Marketing mobile report controls fit the viewport; wide report tables scroll in their own region. Today role rail is intentionally scrollable at constrained widths.

Diagnostic captures `sales-reviewed-report.jpg`, `accounting-close-persisted.jpg` (loading), `company-switch-final.jpg` (false cash priority), `cash-detail-exception-diagnostic.jpg` (unsupported zero exception) and `sales-today-mobile-final.jpg` (actual desktop viewport) are excluded from final approval evidence. The final header/cash captures use a fresh fixture; earlier action/review captures and hashed API results belong to the first fixture session. Repeated names in the chronological capture manifest are earlier observations; final indexed files and `browser-final-checks.json` govern acceptance.
