# Deterministic strategic scenarios

P27 adds a Finance-owned sensitivity model over an approved retained P26 annual plan and an immutable P23 Finance forecast. The service reuses both owners through Application contracts; it never writes forecasts, actuals, budgets, approvals, payments or initiatives. The Domain calculation is `strategic-scenario.v1`.

Revenue source amounts use native revenue credit signs; operating expenses use native expense signs. The chosen forecast must be unfiltered by account/cost center, within the annual fiscal year, with the same explicit currency. Month-start fiscal calendars are supported; no FX or implicit prorating. Known retained actual/forecast values form partial source totals. Missing values and coverage are captured in provenance. The user must choose an explicit source-to-annual multiplier, demand/capacity unit, owner and every numeric assumption, including zero cash inputs.

For each fiscal-year offset, demand and capacity grow by their annual percentages. Fulfilled units are the smaller of demand and capacity. Price starts at scaled source revenue divided by base demand, then grows by price percentage. Revenue equals fulfilled units times price. Operating cost is fixed source cost plus fulfilled units times variable unit cost, both grown by the cost percentage. The variable cost share divides the scaled expense baseline into fixed and per-unit costs.

Collections equal same-year revenue times collection share plus opening receivables. Payments equal same-year operating cost times payment share plus opening payables. Remaining receivables/payables settle fully the following year. Closing cash equals opening cash plus collections minus payments minus additional cash investment plus assumed funding. Closing cash rolls into the next opening cash. Funding gap equals the positive shortfall below the assumed cash floor. Capacity shortfall excludes unmet demand from revenue.

Money rounds to two decimals and units to four, away from zero; rounded balances carry forward. The horizon is 3–10 years, growth is −100% to 300%, shares are 0–1, source scaling is greater than zero through 12×, base demand/capacity are bounded at one billion units, and calculated quantities/cash are bounded at one trillion. Missing or out-of-range inputs block preview. Investments are incremental cash amounts excluded from operating costs; annual plan investments are not added automatically, which avoids double counting. There is no tax, interest, depreciation, seasonality, default, monthly timing or FX model. Scenario funding grants no execution authority.

SQL migration `20261006160024_AddStrategicScenarios` creates four company-owned tables. Header/owned driver columns, annual cash rows, checkpoint bindings and output columns are relational. Source JSON contains bounded frozen provenance, classifications and native dependencies; it is not the sole representation of core inputs or outputs. Company-composite source/parent/initiative FKs, unique request/series revision/predecessor indexes and transactional audit enforce isolation and append-only saves. SQL concurrency has one winner. Idempotent commands return the same authorized version; altered request identity content rejects.

Open reauthorizes both native sources, then reproduces from frozen inputs and outputs. Source checksums, annual fingerprint, calculation version and a canonical decimal checksum must agree. Decimal formatting differences between SQLite/SQL Server cannot change the checksum. Live actuals, owner display-name changes and new plan revisions do not rewrite saved scenario numbers or checkpoint owner names. A source's current read permission remains required; a historical approved-at-capture baseline is not current spending authority. New previews require a currently approved annual baseline.

Duplicate creates a distinct series with a retained origin; edits create a successor. Compare requires identical annual/Finance versions and captured revenue/expense baselines, currency, capacity unit and horizon. Comparison deltas come from saved backend results, and owner/checkpoint/rationale changes are disclosed. History scans 20 records per page, excludes inaccessible source bindings, and retains an explicit next-page flag even when a page contains hidden records. The Finance picker shows the latest 20 native versions; existing scenarios keep their exact older source choice.

The native Overview `period=multiyear` and `/dashboard/planning/scenarios` share one workspace. Company membership guards, typed company/version checks, cancellation and input-revision checks clear revoked or late data. Desktop/narrow layouts, a cash trajectory chart, scrollable numeric tables, formulas, source returns and checkpoint owners follow reference 07 and the two saved P27 references. No AI-generated numeric outcome or static reference image is used in production.
