# P06 implementation

2026-10-02; baseline `37834c7f` plus preserved P01–P05 working-tree changes. Implements P06 from `vcscreens-prompts.md` under its shared contract, C1/C2/C4/C7 current-code entries and repository/scoped instructions. P04 status, implementation, verification, UAT, issue ledger and profile were read; P05 continuity evidence was also read. No commit, deployment or production data change is part of this task.

Finance Today now shows dated outstanding invoices and bills, due-date priorities, separate-currency cash availability, reconciliation exceptions and direct report/payment/close/statement links. It preserves the P03 budget evidence distinction and actual assigned-agent context. Multiple cash currencies are not added into a single headline. The retained cash page suppresses its legacy mixed-currency aggregate display and links the new currency-separated forecast.

The three report routes are `/finance/receivables-aging`, `/finance/payables-aging` and `/finance/cash-forecast`. `FinanceOperationalReportService` lives in the owning Finance module and reuses `FinancialReportSuiteCalculator` aging/rounding, `IFinanceReadService` cash evidence and `FinanceRecordSourcePolicy`. The scoped `FinanceOperationalReportsController` enforces FinanceView and company context. The typed Web client uses company transport and correlation. No entity, EF model, migration, ledger engine, approval engine, outbox or payment dispatcher was added or replaced.

Report meaning is explicit: current retained positive booked obligations issued/received before the end of the UTC cutoff day, less current paid amounts. Paid, cancelled, credit and simulation records are excluded. This is operational aging, not historical ledger reconstruction; later settlements can change a past-cutoff result. Operational/Fortnox source selection uses the same policy as record review. A 5,000-record bound fails visibly instead of silently truncating totals.

Forecast is owning cash at cutoff plus the included remaining receivables due through the 1–30-day horizon, less equivalent payables. Overdue amounts are assumed collected/paid in full during the horizon. No FX, probability weighting, future sales, unrecorded recurring expense or deduction for pending payment instructions is introduced. Each currency is calculated separately. Missing starting cash and projected cash remain unavailable, never zero. Retained balance/opening dates and evidence basis are shown; stale/opening-only cash creates a visible coverage note. CSV uses the same source rows, filters, timestamps, meanings, assumptions, gaps and horizon. Download rereads authorization and current data before the existing Blob module handoff and escapes formula-leading cells.

`FinanceJourneyRoutes` validates company-local report/list/record origins and carries the exact Overview origin, priority/health origin and Finance source selection. Invoice, bill, payment, transaction, review, cash and reconciliation paths use that context; filters remain in URLs. Canonical Work approval review now accepts a validated Finance return, alongside the preserved Sales return. FinancePageBase and report reads guard late/context-changing responses. Offline, malformed response, timeout, invalid filter, denied access, missing evidence, empty scope and download failures have visible states.

Browser UAT found and fixed two functional gaps: new reports initially ignored the configured Finance source filter, and retained Finance review/filter pages lacked interactive render modes. Source scope is now explicit and durable; those pages run their existing server handlers. Invoice status save/reload and policy refusal were exercised without bypassing the existing approval owner. Statement/close calculations, permissions, locking/reopening and separate income/balance presentation remain owned by P07/current modules.

The same review corrected a misleading retained bill provider claim: local booked status or a payment proposal alone no longer completes Sent to Fortnox. Imported provider source or recorded successful provider action supplies that evidence. Expense availability wording for manual records identifies retained booking records. Regression fixtures that actually represent Fortnox bills now declare their source; their existing approval/registration assertions remain intact. Narrow shared Today metadata also wraps, fixing measured mobile overflow without changing desktop layout or earlier role workflows.
