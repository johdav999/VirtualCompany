# Product profile

- Surface: Sales presentation preparation workspace
- Route: `/app/sales/meeting-invitations/{invitationId}/prepare`
- Primary user: sales organizer preparing an agent-led meeting
- Core flow: scheduled invitation -> saved meeting session -> processed active deck -> presenter controls
- Recovery flow: deal without identified company -> link company -> presentation readiness unlocks
- Required relationship: the lead, deal, or contact must resolve to one customer company before a meeting session can be created
