# Product profile: responsibility settings and onboarding

## Product and user

- Product: Virtual Company responsibility assignment settings and company onboarding.
- Primary users: company owners and administrators configuring the operating model; active members inspecting responsibility context.
- Primary job: make the responsible person, compatible agent, authority level, approval-policy summary, escalation route, and executive oversight explicit for six core areas.
- Critical boundary: responsibility controls relevance and routing only. API authorization, approval policy, and tool availability remain independent enforcement boundaries.

## Happy path

1. Choose a micro, small, or medium company pattern during onboarding.
2. Complete onboarding and apply the safe fill-missing preset without overwriting explicit assignments.
3. If member or agent choices are ambiguous, continue to Responsibility assignments.
4. Preview additions, retained assignments, and replacements; explicitly confirm replacements.
5. Edit one area using active same-company members and compatible active agents.
6. Save and follow the refreshed Today link.

## Acceptance targets

- Owners/admins receive backend-derived edit controls; other active members receive the same matrix read-only.
- Preset preview is required before apply and replacement requires explicit confirmation.
- Empty, unassigned, inactive, missing-agent, missing-escalation, error, and success states are visible.
- Desktop uses a matrix plus edit panel; narrow layouts stack cards and controls without a wide table.
- Form controls and actions remain at least 44 px tall on narrow screens; headings, landmarks, labels, status, and errors are semantic.
- English and Swedish localization keys remain paired.
