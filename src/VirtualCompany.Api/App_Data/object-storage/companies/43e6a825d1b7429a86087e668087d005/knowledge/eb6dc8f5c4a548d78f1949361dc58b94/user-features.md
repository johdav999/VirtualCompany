# Virtual Company — User Feature List

## Executive control

- View a company dashboard with key performance indicators, priorities, issues, approvals, and recent activity.
- See a consolidated view of business health across finance, sales, marketing, operations, and support.
- Review and approve decisions that require executive oversight.
- Track important actions and outcomes through an auditable activity history.

## AI agent team

- Work with specialised AI colleagues, including executive, finance, sales, marketing, operations, HR, support, and governance roles.
- Give an agent a goal, ask questions, review its recommendations, and guide its work in a conversation.
- Configure agent responsibilities, autonomy, and the types of decisions that require approval.
- See each agent's current work, recommendations, and relevant business context.

## Guided workshops

- Run structured workshops for important company activities, such as market strategy, business planning, hiring, and operational improvement.
- Capture workshop answers as a shared company draft rather than losing them in a chat.
- Ask agents to research a topic while continuing the workshop conversation.
- Receive cited research findings and proposed updates for review when the research is ready.
- Resume a workshop later and see its history, status, and outstanding decisions.

## Finance

- Review financial performance, forecasts, cash position, and financial priorities.
- Manage finance-related work and approvals with a dedicated finance agent.
- Connect accounting information and review synchronisation activity where integrations are enabled.
- Identify financial risks, follow-up actions, and decisions requiring attention.

## Sales and marketing

- Develop market strategy, positioning, target segments, and campaign ideas with guided assistance.
- Track sales opportunities, customer relationships, and follow-up work.
- Create and review marketing plans, content, and campaign activities.
- Use research and business context to support commercial decisions.

## Support and communication

- Manage customer support work and follow-up actions.
- Send and track business communications where email integrations are configured.
- Keep customer-relevant information, tasks, and decisions connected to the company context.

## Work management and governance

- Create, assign, prioritise, and track tasks across the virtual company.
- Maintain a company knowledge base containing decisions, plans, and operating context.
- Review audit records for material actions, approvals, and automated activity.
- Use role-based access and company isolation so people only see and act on the company information they are entitled to access.
- Monitor integrations, background work, and operational issues so failures can be investigated and resolved.
